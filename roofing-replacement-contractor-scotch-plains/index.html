Scope, schedule, and cost factors

A replacement project generally involves reviewing the existing roof, agreeing on a work scope and materials, and planning the removal and installation. The timing varies with the size and complexity of the roof, access, material availability, and conditions on the job. Have the contractor explain the proposed schedule for your particular project rather than relying on a general timeline.

Key facts for planning:
- What it covers: The written scope should identify the area being replaced, materials, and related work included in the project.
- Typical timing: Project duration depends on the roof’s size and layout, access, materials, and other job conditions.
- Cost in general: The amount depends on the project’s size, material selection, removal needs, access, and any additional work identified during assessment. Request a detailed written estimate to understand what is included.

A few terms may come up. Tear-off means removing existing roofing material. Underlayment is a protective layer installed beneath the outer roofing material. Roof deck is the base surface supporting the roof covering. Ask the contractor to explain whether and how these parts apply to your project.
