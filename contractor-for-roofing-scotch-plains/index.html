Scope, timing, costs, and useful terms

A roofing contractor’s work begins with understanding the roof and the request. Depending on the situation, the discussion may cover inspection, possible repairs or replacement, materials, and how the work will be organized. Ask for the proposed tasks in writing so you can review them carefully.

Key facts to keep in mind
- What it covers: The scope may describe inspection, repair or installation tasks, materials, removal, cleanup, and any work that is not included. Confirm which items apply to your project.
- Typical timing: Timing depends on the type and size of the job, scheduling, weather, materials, and whether concealed damage changes the work. Ask when the contractor expects to begin and how updates will be shared.
- General cost factors: The amount and type of work, material choices, roof access, removal, and underlying repairs can influence cost. No two scopes are automatically alike, so compare the details rather than just the bottom line.
- Comparing providers: Ask each contractor to explain the same concern or requested work. Review the material descriptions, exclusions, schedule, cleanup plan, and payment terms before deciding.
